# Code of Conduct

AI Image Studio follows the Contributor Covenant principles. Participants must communicate respectfully, avoid harassment and focus criticism on work rather than people.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, discrimination, threats, publishing private information, deliberate disruption and misuse of customer media or personal data.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
